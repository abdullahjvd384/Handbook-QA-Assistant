SEV DEFINITIONS

SEV1  Customer-facing outage, data loss, or robot safety fault. Acknowledge within 15 minutes. Incident commander required. Status page update required. Post-incident review required.
SEV2  Major degradation or a single large customer down. Acknowledge within 45 minutes. Incident channel required. Post-incident review required.
SEV3  Minor degradation with a workaround. Acknowledge within 4 hours. Ticket required.
SEV4  Cosmetic or low-impact. Business hours. Ticket required.

Examples
- Payments API returning errors for all customers: SEV1.
- Fleet console slow for one customer: SEV2.
- Dashboard chart mislabelled: SEV4.
