# Parental Leave Policy

## Birth parent leave
Birth parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave, which runs concurrently with any FMLA entitlement. Up to four weeks may be taken before the expected due date.

## Secondary caregiver leave
Fathers and secondary caregivers receive **12 weeks** of fully paid leave, to be taken within six months of the birth or adoption.

## Adoption and foster placement
Adoptive and foster parents receive the same entitlements as birth parents. The primary caregiver is agreed with People Operations before the leave starts.

## Notice
Give your manager and People Operations at least eight weeks' notice where possible.
