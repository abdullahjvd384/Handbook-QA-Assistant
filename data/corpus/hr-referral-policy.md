# Employee Referral Policy

Refer a candidate through the Referrals tab in the HR portal before they apply. If your referral is hired into a full-time role and completes 90 days of employment, you receive a referral bonus of **$3,000** with the next payroll.

Referrals for intern and contract roles earn half the bonus. Members of the Talent Acquisition team, hiring managers for the role, and executives are not eligible. The bonus is taxable income.
