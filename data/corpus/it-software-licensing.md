# Software Licensing Policy

Use software only under a licence the company holds. Open-source dependencies in product code must have a licence on the approved list (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, MPL-2.0); GPL-family licences need Legal review before use. Paid tools are requested through the IT portal and renewed annually by IT. Personal licences may not be used for company work.
