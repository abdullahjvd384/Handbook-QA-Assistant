# Grievance Policy

Raise a grievance in writing to People Operations. You will receive an acknowledgement within two working days and a meeting within ten working days. Outcomes are communicated in writing. Appeals go to the Head of People within five working days of the outcome. Harassment complaints are handled by People Operations and Legal under the Anti-Harassment Policy, separately and confidentially.
