# Performance Review Process

Reviews run once a year with the cycle opening in **April**. Each employee writes a self-review, collects feedback from three to five peers, and meets their manager for a calibration discussion.

Ratings use a four-point scale. Promotion cases are presented by the manager at the department calibration meeting and need agreement from the department head. Compensation changes resulting from the cycle apply from July 1.

Mid-year check-ins are informal and are not rated.
