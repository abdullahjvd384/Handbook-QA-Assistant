# Robotics Firmware Team

The Robotics Firmware Team builds the on-robot control software for the Northgate arm and the mobile base. **Engineering Manager: Aisha Ali**. Team channel: #firmware. The team works from the Austin hardware lab three days a week and owns the hardware-in-the-loop test benches described in the CI Pipeline Guide.
