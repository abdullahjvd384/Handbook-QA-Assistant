# Security Incident Reporting

If you suspect a security incident, such as a phishing email you clicked, a lost device, or unexpected access to a system, report it immediately to security@northgate.example and post in #security-incidents. Do not try to investigate on your own. The Security team will acknowledge within one hour during business hours and will tell you what to preserve. Reporting early is never penalised.
