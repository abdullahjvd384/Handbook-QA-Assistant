Customer Support Escalation

Support handles customer tickets in the shared inbox. Technical escalations go to the owning team's on-call through the paging tool, never by direct message.

Status page

Only the Support lead and the incident commander may publish status page updates. Engineers draft the wording in the incident channel; Support publishes it. Updates during a SEV1 go out at least hourly.

Customer credits

Service credits for outages are approved by the Head of Product and processed by Finance.
