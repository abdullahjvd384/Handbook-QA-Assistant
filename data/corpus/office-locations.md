# Office Locations

Northgate Robotics has two offices.

- **Austin, Texas** (headquarters): 4200 Burnet Road. Hardware lab on the ground floor, engineering on floors two and three. Open 07:00 to 22:00 with badge access.
- **Denver, Colorado**: 1700 Platte Street. Data and Product teams, plus a small demo lab. Open 08:00 to 21:00.

Both offices have visitor registration at reception. Guests must be accompanied in lab areas. There are no other company offices; employees elsewhere work remotely under the Remote Work Policy.
