# Email and Messaging Guidelines

Use Slack for day-to-day communication and email for anything external or anything you may need to find a year later. Keep customer conversations in the shared support inbox. Do not forward company email to personal accounts. Direct messages in Slack are not a system of record: decisions go in channels or documents.
