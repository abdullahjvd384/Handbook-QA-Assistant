# Benefits Overview

Medical, dental and vision insurance for employees and dependents, with the company paying 90 percent of the employee premium. 401(k) with a 4 percent match. Annual wellness allowance of $300. Home office allowance of $1,000 after the introductory period. Conference and training budget of $3,000 per year under the Training and Conference Policy. Employee referral bonus under the Referral Policy. On-call stipend for engineers on rotation.
