# Lab Safety Rules

Closed shoes and safety glasses in the hardware lab at all times. Robot arms under test run inside the marked cells with the light curtain active; never reach into a cell while the arm is powered. Visitors stay behind the yellow line. Report near-misses to the lab manager the same day. Food and drink stay outside the lab.
