# Onboarding Checklist

Week one: collect your laptop from IT, set up SSO and multi-factor authentication, install the VPN client, read the Code of Conduct, the Password Policy and the Security Incident Reporting process, and meet your onboarding buddy. Week two: read your team's runbooks, shadow a stand-up, and complete the security training. Month one: first one-to-one with your manager about introductory period goals; the introductory period is described in the Introductory Period Policy. Handbook last reviewed 2026-10-01.
