# WFH Equipment Policy

After the introductory period you may claim up to **$1,000** once for a desk, chair, monitor or similar home office equipment. Claim it through the expense system with receipts; it is approved by your manager and is not subject to the VP threshold. Equipment bought under this allowance stays with you. Replacement allowances are considered after four years.
