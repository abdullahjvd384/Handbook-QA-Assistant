# Handbook FAQ

**Where do I request leave?** In the HR portal. See the Leave Policy for entitlements.

**How do I book travel?** Through VoyageHub. Bookings outside the tool need a department head exception.

**Who do I ask for a new laptop?** Raise a request in the IT portal; your team's engineering manager approves it.

**When do I get reimbursed?** Within the Reimbursement SLA after final approval, with the next payroll.

**Can I work from another country?** No. See the Remote Work Policy.

**Where are decisions recorded?** In channels and documents, not direct messages. See Email and Messaging Guidelines.
