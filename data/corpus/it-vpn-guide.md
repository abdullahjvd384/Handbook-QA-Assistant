# VPN Guide

The VPN client is installed on every company laptop. Sign in with your SSO credentials and a hardware key or authenticator app.

- You may hold at most **2 concurrent VPN sessions**; a further device will be refused.
- The VPN is required for any access to internal dashboards, the robot fleet console, and the code hosting platform from outside the office network.
- Split tunnelling is enabled: only internal ranges go over the VPN.
- If the client shows "certificate expired", run Self Service > Renew VPN Certificate. Contact IT if that fails.
