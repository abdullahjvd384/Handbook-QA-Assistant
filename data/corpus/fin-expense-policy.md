# Expense Policy

## 1. Principles
Spend company money as carefully as you would spend your own. Claim only what you spent, attach a receipt, and submit within 30 days of the expense.

## 2. What you can claim
Business meals with clients, software subscriptions approved by IT, books and online courses under the Training Policy, local travel, and business travel costs within the limits set by the Travel Policy.

## 3. Travel limits
Flight, hotel and per-diem limits for business travel are defined in **section 4.2 of the Travel Policy**. This document does not restate them; Finance will reject a travel claim that exceeds those limits unless an exception was recorded in VoyageHub.

## 4. Approval thresholds
- Claims up to $2,500 are approved by your direct manager.
- Claims **above $2,500 require approval from a Vice President** in addition to your manager, **except travel that was booked through VoyageHub**, which is pre-approved at booking time and needs only manager sign-off at claim time.
- Any single claim above $10,000 must go through Procurement rather than expenses.

## 5. Reimbursement
Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run. See the Reimbursement SLA for the processing time commitment.

## 6. Non-reimbursable
Alcohol outside client entertainment, traffic and parking fines, personal upgrades, and expenses submitted more than 30 days late.
