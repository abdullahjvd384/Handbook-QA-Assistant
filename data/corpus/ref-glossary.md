# Glossary

- **CI**: Continuous Integration. The automated pipeline that tests every change.
- **HIL**: Hardware-in-the-loop testing, where firmware runs on a real controller connected to simulated sensors.
- **IC**: Incident Commander. The person coordinating an active incident.
- **PIR**: Post-Incident Review. The blameless write-up after a SEV1 or SEV2.
- **RPO**: Recovery Point Objective. The maximum acceptable amount of data loss measured in time, for example 15 minutes of writes.
- **RTO**: Recovery Time Objective. The maximum acceptable time to restore a service after a disaster. Not to be confused with return-to-office arrangements, which are covered by the Remote Work Policy.
- **SEV**: Severity level of an incident, from SEV1 (most severe) to SEV4.
- **SSO**: Single Sign-On through the company identity provider.
- **PO**: Purchase Order raised by Procurement.
- **MDM**: Mobile Device Management, used to enrol personal phones under the BYOD Policy.
