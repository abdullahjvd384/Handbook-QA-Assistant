# Laptop and Hardware Policy

## 1. Standard issue
Every employee receives a company laptop on day one. Engineers receive a 16 GB or 32 GB model depending on role. The laptop remains company property.

## 2. Replacement
Laptops are refreshed after **3 years**. Early replacement for damage, theft or performance problems is requested through the IT portal.

**Approval for a replacement comes from the employee's team engineering manager**; IT does not approve replacements itself. Once approved, IT fulfils the request within **3 business days** in Austin and Denver, and ships to remote employees within a further two days.

## 3. Loss or theft
Report a lost or stolen laptop to IT and the Security team within 24 hours so that it can be wiped remotely. File a police report for theft; a copy is needed for the insurance claim.

## 4. Peripherals
Monitors, keyboards and docks are provided in the office. Home peripherals are claimed under the WFH Equipment Policy.

## 5. Software
Install software only from the Self Service catalogue or with an approved exception under the Software Licensing Policy.
