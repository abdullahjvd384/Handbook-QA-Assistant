# Working Hours and Holidays

Standard working hours are 9:00 to 18:00 local time, Monday to Friday, with core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 Central Time during which everyone is expected to be reachable. The company observes 11 paid holidays per year; the dated list is in the Holiday Calendar CSV. Optional floating holidays: two per year, chosen by the employee.

Hardware lab staff follow a shift roster published by the lab manager.
