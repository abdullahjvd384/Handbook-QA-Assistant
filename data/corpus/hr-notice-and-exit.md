# Resignation Notice and Exit Process

Employment is at will. When resigning, employees are asked to give **4 weeks' notice** so that work can be handed over; during the introductory period one week is sufficient. Managers may release an employee earlier, in which case the notice period is paid out.

Before the last working day: return your laptop and access badge to IT, hand over documents to your manager, and complete the exit survey. The final paycheck is issued in line with state law, and unused leave is paid out where the state requires it.
