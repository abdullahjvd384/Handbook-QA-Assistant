# Bring Your Own Device Policy

Personal phones may access email and Slack after enrolling in mobile device management, which lets IT wipe company data only. Personal laptops may not access source code, customer data or the fleet console. Lost personal devices with company data must be reported under the Security Incident Reporting process.
