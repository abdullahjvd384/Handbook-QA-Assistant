# Leave Policy v1

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out paid time off entitlements for full-time employees of Northgate Robotics in the United States.

## 2. Annual leave
Full-time employees accrue **12 days of annual leave** per calendar year, credited each pay period in equal parts. Leave is requested through the HR portal at least three working days in advance for absences longer than one day.

## 3. Sick leave
Employees receive 8 days of sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required for absences of three or more consecutive days.

## 4. Carry forward
Up to 8 unused annual leave days may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried days must be used by March 31 or they lapse.

## 5. Company holidays
The company observes 11 paid holidays per year. The list is published each December in the Working Hours and Holidays document.

## 6. Approval
Leave is approved by the employee's direct manager. Leave of more than ten consecutive working days also needs the department head's approval.
