# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees past the introductory period may work remotely up to **2 days per week** by default. Teams that run physical hardware labs set their own in-office days; check with your manager.

## Equipment
A one-time home office allowance of $1,000 is available after the introductory period. Claim it under the WFH Equipment Policy.

## Expectations
Be reachable on Slack during core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 Central Time. Keep your calendar accurate. Use the VPN for any access to internal systems, see the VPN Guide.

## Working from another state
Working from a state other than Texas or Colorado for more than two weeks needs manager approval and a note to People Operations for payroll tax purposes. Working from outside the United States is not supported.
