# Reimbursement SLA

Finance processes an approved expense claim within **5 business days** of the final approval and pays it with the next payroll run. Claims returned for missing receipts restart the clock when resubmitted. Per-diem allowances are paid in advance when the trip is booked in VoyageHub; no claim is needed for them.
