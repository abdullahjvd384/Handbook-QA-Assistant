# VoyageHub Booking Guide

1. Sign in to VoyageHub with SSO.
2. Enter the trip purpose and the cost center; your manager is notified automatically and approves in the tool.
3. Choose flights and hotels within the limits shown. Options outside policy are greyed out; an exception request can be raised in the tool and goes to the department head.
4. Per-diem is calculated from your grade and the destination's city tier and paid before the trip.
5. Receipts for hotel stays are attached in VoyageHub, not in the expense system, so travel booked in VoyageHub does not need a separate expense claim or VP approval.
