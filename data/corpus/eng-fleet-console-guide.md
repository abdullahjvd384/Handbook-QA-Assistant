# Fleet Console Guide

The fleet console shows every deployed robot's status, firmware version and last telemetry heartbeat. Access requires the VPN and the fleet-operator role granted through the Access Request Process. Firmware rollouts are staged by customer site and can be paused from the console. Emergency stop commands from the console are logged and reviewed weekly by the Robotics Firmware lead.
