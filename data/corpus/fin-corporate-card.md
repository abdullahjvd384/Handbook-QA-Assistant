# Corporate Card Policy

Corporate cards are issued to managers and to frequent travellers on request. The monthly limit is $10,000. Every transaction needs a receipt uploaded within seven days. Personal spend on the card is a policy breach even if repaid. Lost cards are reported to Finance and the bank the same day.
